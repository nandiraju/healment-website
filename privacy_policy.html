Privacy Policy

Plain words about how HealMent treats your information.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Your journal stays on your phone

HealMent keeps what you write on your own device. We do not run servers that store your entries, and you do not need to create an account to use the app.

What is stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • Your journal entries and notes
  • Your mood choices and the charts built from them
  • Prompts you create and items you bookmark
  • Your settings, such as dark or light theme

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or advertising ID through the app. We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads based on what you write.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It may use cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device type and the pages you view. It does not see anything you write in the app, because your journal stays on your phone. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings. When you tap a button to download the app, you go to the App Store or Google Play, which have their own privacy policies.

Keeping your data safe

Your entries are protected by the security of your phone. Use a screen lock and keep your phone updated. Because the data lives only on your device, deleting the app or losing your phone can remove your entries. Back up your phone regularly if you want to keep them.

Your choices

You are in control of your entries. You can read, change or delete them inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your phone.

Children

HealMent is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

If you have a question about privacy, please visit our contact page.
